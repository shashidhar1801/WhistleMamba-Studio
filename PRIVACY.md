# Privacy

The browser sends each selected WAV to a service on the same computer. The service works on a temporary local copy; opening or analyzing a WAV does not upload it to an external service or rewrite the original recording.

Closing the browser tab leaves the service and its workspace running. Download your results before choosing **Remove session copy** or **Exit Studio**. The workspace is temporary, not a backup. Downloaded archives and your original WAV are separate files. Display preferences may remain in the browser, and the Windows launcher writes local operational logs.

An exported project can contain a local audio path, file and model hashes, predictions, review states, edits and notes. The export manifest can also contain temporary paths. Inspect these details before sharing an archive; the WAV itself is not embedded. Keep the original WAV separately.
